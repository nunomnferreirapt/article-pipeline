# Brief

- Topic: Why small field teams adopt offline-first apps
- Audience: people who run or equip field crews of two to six people and are weighing a tooling change
- Angle: adoption is a response to lost evenings, not a love of technology; name the limits as plainly as the gains
- Voice: plain, direct, short sentences; no marketing words
- Length: 700 to 1,000 words

## Allowed sources

Only the sources listed in `sources.md` may be used. Nothing else, not even
general knowledge, enters the draft as a factual claim.

All three sources in this example are invented. They exist to show the
pipeline's file formats and to give the gate something to check.

## Research angles

1. facts-and-figures: numbers, dates, definitions
2. practitioner-experience: what people who did it report
3. counter-arguments-and-limits: where it fails, what the critics say
