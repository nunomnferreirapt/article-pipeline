# Research: facts and figures

Angle: numbers, dates, definitions. Sources used: S1 only; S2 and S3 carry no
figures beyond those already in S1.

- Claim: The survey covered 140 small crews over four months in 2025.
  - Source: [S1]
  - Quote: "Between March and June 2025 we surveyed 140 small field crews."
- Claim: A small crew means two to six people on site.
  - Source: [S1]
  - Quote: "A crew counted as small when it had two to six people on site."
- Claim: Most crews are offline for at least an hour on a normal day.
  - Source: [S1]
  - Quote: "Of the 140 crews surveyed, 87 reported at least one offline hour on a typical working day."
- Claim: The median crew is offline three hours a day.
  - Source: [S1]
  - Quote: "The median crew spent 3 hours a day without a usable connection."
- Claim: Definition of offline-first used by the survey.
  - Source: [S1]
  - Quote: "stores every record on the device first and syncs when a connection returns"
- Claim: Sync time fell from 3.5 hours to 40 minutes after the switch.
  - Source: [S1]
  - Quote: "Crews that moved to offline-first tools cut their end-of-day sync time from 3.5 hours to 40 minutes on average."
- Claim: 62 percent of 2024 adopters were still using the tool a year later.
  - Source: [S1]
  - Quote: "Of the crews that adopted an offline-first tool in 2024, 62 percent were still using it a year later."
- Claim: Nineteen crews abandoned the tool, mainly over conflicts, stale data and audit trails.
  - Source: [S1]
  - Quote: "The reasons given most often were sync conflicts between devices, stale reference data, and a lack of an audit trail that their client would accept."
