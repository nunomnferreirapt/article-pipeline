# Research: counter-arguments and limits

Angle: where it fails, what the critics say. Sources used: S3 mainly, S1 and
S2 where they confirm a limit.

- Claim: Sync conflicts are the normal case, not an edge case.
  - Source: [S3]
  - Quote: "Sync conflicts are not an edge case for field crews. They are the normal case whenever two people measure the same thing on the same day."
- Claim: Small teams have nobody to resolve conflicts.
  - Source: [S3]
  - Quote: "A small team has nobody whose job it is to resolve those conflicts, so they get resolved by whoever happens to sync last."
- Claim: The crew lead hit exactly that problem.
  - Source: [S2]
  - Quote: "The first time two devices edited the same record we got a conflict screen that nobody understood."
- Claim: Reference data goes stale without anyone noticing.
  - Source: [S3]
  - Quote: "The app does not know the list is old, and neither does the person using it."
- Claim: Audit trails have a blind spot before sync.
  - Source: [S3]
  - Quote: "The hours before sync are a blind spot. Offline-first is not a free lunch in regulated settings."
- Claim: Offline support doubles the test surface.
  - Source: [S3]
  - Quote: "Teams of two to six people rarely have the budget to test both paths on every release."
- Claim: The essay does not argue for staying on paper.
  - Source: [S3]
  - Quote: "None of this means small teams should stay on paper."
